## Goal
Make the simulation and the daily run more realistic and safer without changing any
strategy's rules, so no new trial is added to the deflated Sharpe count (60 so far).
Background: the review of the "Jeron v2.0" upgrade spec (project files,
`jeron/v2-spec-review/review.md`). Most of that spec's strategy and exit changes are
deferred, because they were designed by looking at the losing years and the holdout.

## Part 1 (this PR)
1. **2026 NSE holidays** in `calendar/nse_holidays.csv`. Without them every 2026
   holiday was treated as a trading day (Dussehra is Tuesday 20 Oct). The 12 holidays
   up to 16 Oct and the Budget-day Sunday session on 1 Feb were checked against the
   days NSE published a bhavcopy (`verified = yes`); the four still ahead (20 Oct,
   10 Nov, 24 Nov, 25 Dec) are from the circular, `verified = no`.
2. **Gap-up fill** (`engine-v3`). A buy that opens above the entry zone is skipped. Up
   to `engine-v2` it filled at the top of the zone if the day traded down to it, which
   on daily bars buys the gaps that fail and misses the ones that run. The zone stays
   at 0.5 x ATR.
3. **No entry in a 2% or 5% price band** (`engine-v3`, `PortfolioRules.min_band_pct`).
   Such a stock can lock at the lower circuit for days, so a 1R stop can't fill. It is
   an entry rule in the engine rather than a universe rule in the scan, because the
   scan's universe sets the relative-strength ranks that tech-v1 and every strategy
   use; changing it would change every strategy. The band is stored per day from NSE's
   security list (from 2020); earlier days have no band and pass.
4. **Quality check #9, unexplained drops.** A universe stock that falls more than 15%
   with no split or bonus, not locked at the lower circuit and not on its first trade,
   is WARNed. Its signals that day are sent marked HELD, and the next pre-open check
   lists its position under CHECK BY HAND instead of an action, because an unadjusted
   demerger or rights issue would trip the stop. The existing `big_moves` check (20%,
   either way) is unchanged.
5. **Missed runs** (Jeron runs on my computer). A job missed while the computer slept
   still runs when it wakes: the pre-open check until about 15:30, the daily run until
   the next morning (was one hour). When the scheduler starts, `catch_up` runs the daily
   job if the latest session has no scan (and weekly if that session ended the week),
   and the pre-open check during market hours.

Paper accounts already open keep `engine-v2`'s fill and no band rule (their stored
rules predate them), so their replays don't change. Accounts opened from `engine-v3`
backtests use the new rules.

## Part 1b: execution helpers (no strategy changes)
- **Idle cash:** capital minus open positions at cost minus the next session's live
  signals not yet taken. Above ₹50,000, /portfolio, Today and the 08:30 check say to park
  it in a liquid fund; below zero, to free some up. The backtests credit idle cash with
  6.5% a year, which a broker balance doesn't earn.
- **Placing the buy:** signals and trade plans give the order steps: a limit at the top
  of the entry zone in the 09:00-09:07 pre-open auction (it fills at the auction price,
  NSE's official open, whenever that is at or below the limit), cancelled at 09:15 if it
  didn't fill, then a GTT stop-loss at the stop, which counts during the day.
- **Early warning:** /compare and the weekly summary flag an active strategy whose last
  20 paper trades average below the backtest's 5th percentile for 20 trades. A warning
  only; retirement stays the 50-trade rule.

## Part 2 (later, pre-registered before any run)
- A combined book of `rs-pullback-v1` and `results-drift-v1` with no change to either
  strategy's rules: pre-registered in `docs/plans/combined-book-v1.md`, judged by paper
  trading because the holdout has been used. Built; run it once with
  `backtest --strategy combined-book`.
- Gate 5 for strategies registered from now on as single-regime. `trend-breakout-v1`
  stays failed. Built: a `Strategy` may set `single_regime` (the regimes it trades) only
  with `registered_on` on or after 2026-10-18 (`SINGLE_REGIME_GATE_FROM`); its Gate 5 is
  "positive in each regime it trades, not below 0R in the others" instead of "positive
  in 2 of 3". No registered strategy sets it, so no result changes.
- A Nifty 500 cash-sweep line reported beside the strategy, not inside its gates.
  Built: every backtest report has `cash_sweep` (out-of-sample and holdout curve stats)
  and the equity rows a `sweep` value, drawn dotted on the backtest page. Each session
  the cash held at the previous close earns Nifty 500's return instead of the
  liquid-fund interest, with no switching costs or tax (`stats.cash_sweep_curve`).
  Runs made before it have no such line; re-running a backtest adds it.
