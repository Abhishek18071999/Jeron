# M6: Fundamentals and news brain

Status: planned (branch `claude/jeron-m4-6px1i8`).

## Goal (spec section 11)
Point-in-time fundamentals, results calendar, LLM news labels with my test set;
re-backtest with all three brains.

## Starting point
- No M3 strategy passes section 6 on the technical brain alone, so every signal is
  research only. M6 adds the other two brains and the results blackout, then
  re-backtests to see whether any strategy passes.
- The PR bundle Jeron already downloads every day from NSE's archive also holds that
  day's board-meeting intimations (`bm`) and corporate announcements (`an`), back to
  2016. Each day's file lists what was announced that day, so the history is
  point-in-time without any extra download. (www.nseindia.com turns scripts away, and
  the build container can't reach it; its CSV download still loads with `events-import`.)
- Gap: the `bm` files from April to October 2022 are empty. Backtests say so rather
  than guess.

## Scope, in build order
1. **Results calendar and event blackout** (spec sections 2 and 5).
   - Board meetings from the PR bundle's `bm` file: date and purpose (financial results,
     dividend, fund raising, bonus/split), known from the evening of the day NSE listed
     them. `events --start 2016-01-01` loads the history from the bundles already
     downloaded; `daily` adds each day's. Loaded in the build container: 95,922
     meetings, 79,889 of them for results; Reliance's results dates match its filings.
   - The rule "no new entries from 3 trading days before results until the day after"
     goes into the engine as a portfolio rule, with a test in `test_risk_rules.py`.
     Every signal's `event_risk` field names the next results date.
   - The backtest uses the calendar where it exists and says how much history is
     covered.
2. **Corporate announcements** (the news source).
   - NSE corporate announcements from the PR bundle's `an` file (company, symbol,
     subject, text), keyed by NSE's own symbol, de-duplicated, mutual-fund NAV notices
     dropped. RSS feeds come later, only if the announcements prove too thin.
3. **LLM news labels** (spec section 3).
   - Each announcement gets an event type (results, guidance, order win, regulatory
     action, promoter activity, rating change, management change, litigation, other),
     sentiment from -2 to +2 and a one-line reason, as strict JSON checked against a
     schema. Labels are cached and store the model name and prompt version. The LLM
     never sees or produces prices, targets or stops.
   - Default model: Claude Haiku 4.5 through the Anthropic API. The key lives only in
     `.env` (`JERON_ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`). Without a key, the news brain is off and the
     dashboard says so.
   - Test set: a CSV of ~100 real headlines for me to label by hand. The report gives
     accuracy per event type and the sentiment error.
   - News score: time-decayed by event type, 0-100.
4. **Point-in-time fundamentals** (spec sections 2 and 3).
   - Source to confirm (see below). Every number is keyed by the date it was filed, so a
     backtest on day D sees only what was public on D.
   - Quality score: sales and EPS growth (3y, TTM), ROE, ROCE, debt/equity, interest
     cover, margin trend, operating cash flow vs profit, promoter holding and pledge.
     Valuation: P/E and EV/EBITDA against the stock's own 5-year range and its sector.
   - Hard rejects: pledge above 25% or rising sharply, negative operating cash flow 3
     years running, auditor resignation or qualified opinion in the last 12 months.
5. **Re-backtest with three brains.**
   - New strategy versions that combine the brains with per-tier weights (threshold 80,
     85 in a bad regime), run walk-forward like M3. Out-of-sample, after-cost numbers
     only. A written report either way, as in M3.
   - Where fundamentals or news history is short, the report says which years each brain
     covers instead of filling gaps.

## Decisions for Abhishek
- **Fundamentals source.** Default: NSE's official quarterly results and shareholding
  filings (XBRL). These are point-in-time by filing date, so they can be backtested
  honestly. They take the longest to build, and older filings can be patchy.
  Alternative: a Screener.in CSV export. This is quick, but it is today's snapshot
  only. It would work as a live filter, but a backtest could not use it without
  look-ahead.
- **Anthropic API key.** Needed for news labels. It is paid per use, and labelling a
  day's announcements should cost very little with Haiku. You create the key at
  console.anthropic.com and put it in `.env`.
- **Your test set.** About 100 headlines to label once the announcements are flowing.

## Tests
- Parsers for board meetings, announcements and filings against saved NSE samples.
- The blackout rule in the engine (`test_risk_rules.py`) and in signals.
- LLM labels: schema validation, rejection of bad JSON, caching (a fake model in
  tests; no API calls in CI).
- Point-in-time: a fundamentals query on day D never returns a filing made after D.
- Fundamental score and hard rejects on hand-built companies.
- Re-backtest reproducible; golden trades unchanged for the old strategy versions.

## Acceptance checks
- On the laptop: board meetings, announcements and filings load for the Nifty 500.
- News-label accuracy on my test set is reported.
- A backtest report with all three brains, comparing them against the technical brain
  alone.
