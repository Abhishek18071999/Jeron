# M3: Backtester

Status: merged (PR #4). Real-data results: `docs/backtests/M3-report.md` (no strategy passes).

## Goal (spec section 11)
Engine, Indian costs, walk-forward, report cards, golden tests, first 2-3 strategies
evaluated honestly.
*Accept:* reproducible; at least one strategy passes section 6, or a written report on
why none does.

## Scope
1. **Market data for backtests** (`app/backtest/market.py`, `features.py`): every
   stock's split- and bonus-adjusted bars from the NSE bhavcopy (delisted stocks
   included, renamed stocks joined), aligned on one calendar. The universe rules and
   the `tech-v1` score are the daily scan's, applied to every day with data up to that
   day only. A test checks that the series equal the scan's `snapshot` and `score`.
2. **Engine** (`engine.py`): event-driven, daily bars, long only.
   - Signal at the close; buy at the next open if it is within the entry zone (up to
     the signal close + 0.5 x ATR), otherwise a limit at the top of the zone for that
     day. No buy if the stock opens below the stop, doesn't trade, runs away, or is
     locked at the upper circuit.
   - Stop sells at the stop, or at the open on a gap below it. On a day locked at the
     lower circuit it can't fill and sells at the next open (spec section 2). If the
     stop and the target are both inside a day's range, the stop is assumed first.
   - Spec section 5 exits: book half at T1 (+2R), stop to breakeven at +1R (closing
     basis), trail the rest by 2 x ATR(14) on closing basis, time stop (swing: not +1R
     within 15 sessions; positional: at the 60-session review).
   - Spec section 5 sizing: `shares = floor(equity x risk% / (entry - stop))`, risk 1%,
     at most 20% of equity and 1% of 20-day average volume; heat cap 6%; risk halved
     by the regime filter (Nifty 50 below its 200-day EMA or VIX in its top decile) and
     again in a 10% drawdown; a 15% drawdown pauses new entries for 20 sessions.
   - Spec section 4 checks: stop distance at least 1 x ATR and at most 12% (swing) or
     20% (positional); reward:risk to T2 (+3R) at least 2 after costs.
   - Dividends are paid in cash on ex-dates. A stock that stops trading is sold at its
     last close (flagged). No new entries on days whose data-quality report failed.
3. **Costs** (`costs.py`): Zerodha delivery charges (brokerage 0, STT 0.1% each side,
   NSE transaction charge, SEBI fee, stamp duty 0.015% on buys, GST, DP charge per
   sale) and slippage by 20-day median turnover (0.05% to 0.5% per side). All rates
   are fields of `CostModel`. Estimated post-tax view: STCG/LTCG with the July 2024
   rates, set-off and carry-forward; dividends at a 30% slab.
4. **Walk-forward** (`walkforward.py`): first 252 sessions are warm-up; the latest 12
   months are held out; yearly test windows start once two years of tradable history
   exist, each traded with the grid point that had the best training Sharpe ratio
   (at least 30 trades) on everything before it. One continuous simulation trades
   the test windows and the holdout. Only out-of-sample numbers are reported. Every
   grid point's training numbers are logged per window, and a deflated Sharpe ratio
   accounts for the number of variants tried.
5. **Gates** (spec section 6), on the walk-forward test period: expectancy > 0.15R,
   profit factor >= 1.3, max drawdown <= 20%, >= 100 trades, positive in at least 2 of
   3 regimes, Sharpe above buy-and-hold Nifty 500. Plus: the holdout's expectancy
   must be positive.
6. **Strategies** (`strategies.py`), each with at most 12 grid points:
   | Strategy | Tier | Entry | Grid |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | `score-swing-v1` | swing | tech-v1 score rises to a threshold (85 when risk-off) | threshold 70/75/80/85, stop 1.5/2/2.5 ATR |
   | `breakout-52w-v1` | positional | close above the prior 252-session high, volume >= k x average, above 200-EMA | k 1.5/2, stop 2/3 ATR |
   | `pullback-trend-v1` | swing | uptrend (close > 200-EMA, 20-EMA > 50-EMA, ADX > 20) and RSI(14) back above a level | level 35/40/45, stop 1.5/2 ATR |
7. **Storage, CLI, API, web:** `backtest_runs` (summary, gates, equity curve, data
   fingerprint), `backtest_trades`, `backtest_variants`. Runs are never overwritten.
   `python -m app.cli backtest [--strategy KEY]`. `/backtests`, `/backtests/{id}`,
   `/backtests/{id}/trades`. A Backtests page and a report card per run: verdict and
   gates, test vs holdout vs Nifty 500, equity and drawdown chart, by year and regime,
   exits and skipped signals, tax estimate, worst trades, rules in plain language, every
   variant tried, caveats, and every trade.

## Spec readings
- "Positional review at 60": the backtest exits at the 60-session review if the trade
  hasn't reached +1R (a person reviewing would most likely do the same).
- "15% drawdown: pause new entries and show a review screen": the backtest pauses for
  20 sessions, then resumes at half risk; it pauses again only after a further 5% fall.
  Without a resume rule a flat account could never recover.
- The holdout must have positive expectancy for a strategy to be live-eligible. The
  spec says to hold it out but not what it must show.
- Sector cap and the correlation check need point-in-time sectors, which NSE's archive
  doesn't provide (only today's Nifty 500 industries). They move to M4's risk manager,
  with the rest of the portfolio rules.
- Stops are ATR-based. A structural stop (below the swing low) is an M4 signal option.

## Known gaps
- GSM exclusion starts with NSE's first security list (2020); ASM uses imported lists
  only, so neither is point in time before that. Each report says so.
- Circuit locks are seen only when a whole day trades at one price (high = low).
- The benchmark is the Nifty 500 price index; NSE's archive has no total-return series.
- The backtest's EMAs use all history; the daily scan uses its last 450 sessions, so a
  200-day EMA can differ slightly between the two.

## Tests
Golden engine tests (known bars to known trades and P&L, including costs, gaps, circuit
locks, time stops, sizing caps and the drawdown pause); cost and tax arithmetic; the
backtest's indicator and score series against the scan's; walk-forward folds,
reproducibility, a no-look-ahead check (changing later prices doesn't change earlier
choices), point-in-time universe rules, the deflated Sharpe ratio; a database test that
loads renamed and split stocks, runs a strategy twice with identical results, and
serves it through the API.

## Acceptance checks
- `python -m app.cli backtest` run twice on the same data gives the same fingerprint and
  identical numbers.
- Results on real data and the verdict per strategy: see `docs/backtests/M3-report.md`.
