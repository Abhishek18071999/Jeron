# M4: Signals, risk manager, paper trading

Status: in review (PR for branch `claude/jeron-m4-6px1i8`).

## Goal (spec section 11)
Schema, sizing, portfolio rules, automatic paper trading.
*Accept:* every section 5 rule has a test; paper trades update daily.

## Starting point
M3 found that no strategy passes section 6 (`docs/backtests/M3-report.md`). Ground rule:
no signal without a backtested strategy behind it. So M4 builds the machinery and runs
it on the three M3 strategies, but every signal they produce is marked **research
only**: it is paper-traded to collect forward evidence and is never presented as a trade
to take (M5's alerts will skip it). A strategy whose latest backtest run is
live-eligible produces normal signals with the same code.

## Scope
1. **Risk manager in the engine.** Paper trading uses the backtest engine itself
   (`app/backtest/engine.py`), so a paper trade follows exactly the rules that were
   backtested (spec section 7: "auto paper-traded with the same rules"). New, opt-in
   portfolio rules (off in backtests, so the M3 results and golden trades don't change;
   the engine stays `engine-v2`):
   - sector cap: a stock's sector may hold at most 30% of equity (position values at
     the latest close, plus the new position at the top of its entry zone);
   - correlation check: skip a stock whose daily returns over the last 120 sessions
     have a correlation of 0.7 or more with two stocks already held or ordered that
     day (two banks are allowed, the third is not);
   - max open positions configurable per tier (defaults 5 swing, 8 positional);
   - portfolio heat warning at 5% (recorded per day; entries above 6% are blocked as
     before).
   The engine can also leave positions open at the end and place orders on the last
   day (paper trading needs both), and it records each day's open risk.
2. **Signals** (`app/signals/`): the spec section 4 schema as a Pydantic model; every
   field is required and a signal that doesn't validate is rejected. A signal is built
   from the engine's order (entry zone, stop, shares) and the market data on the day:
   - `why`: 3-5 reasons from the strategy's condition and the indicators that day;
   - `entry_zone`: low = the signal close, high = close + 0.5 x ATR, valid for the next
     session (a lower open still fills if it is above the stop);
   - `stop`: the strategy's ATR stop; `targets`: +2R (T1) and +3R (T2) from the top of
     the zone; reward:risk to T1 and T2 after estimated costs and slippage;
   - `expected_holding`: the tier's range (swing 3-15 sessions, positional 21-126);
   - `conviction` 1-5 from the technical score (below 60: 1, 60s: 2, 70s: 3, 80s: 4,
     90+: 5);
   - `brains_breakdown`: technical = tech-v1 score; fundamental and news are null
     until M6, and combined = technical;
   - `event_risk`: "not checked" until M6 brings the results calendar;
   - `backtest_stats`: the strategy's latest backtest run, out of sample.
   Signals are stored once and never changed (a change would be a new version).
3. **Paper trading** (`app/paper/`): one paper account per strategy version, opened
   with the grid point and settings the latest backtest run chose (kept fixed; new
   parameters mean a new account). Each day after the scan, the account is replayed
   by the engine from its first day to the newest day on the stored data, so the
   result is deterministic and uses the backtested rules; new orders on the last day
   become signals. Stored per account: every signal (immutable), the trades (open and
   closed, re-derived each day), and the daily equity, cash, heat and drawdown. If a
   replay no longer produces a signal recorded earlier (for example after a data
   correction), the day's notes say so.
4. **Settings** (`JERON_` env vars): `CAPITAL` (default 10,00,000), `RISK_PCT` (0.5-2,
   default 1), `MAX_POSITIONS_SWING` (5), `MAX_POSITIONS_POSITIONAL` (8),
   `SECTOR_CAP_PCT` (30).
5. **Jobs and API.** `python -m app.cli paper [--date D] [--strategy KEY]`; `daily` runs
   it after the scan. The paper job refuses to run when the day's scan didn't run (same
   reasons as the scan). API: `/paper/accounts`, `/paper/accounts/{id}`,
   `/signals?date=`, `/signals/{id}`.
6. **Web.** A Paper trading page: each strategy's account with its stage (research
   only / paper), equity against capital, heat, drawdown, open positions with their
   R so far, closed trades, and the latest signals with every schema field.

## Schema changes
`paper_accounts`, `signals`, `paper_trades`, `paper_days` (one migration).

## Spec readings (please confirm)
- Research-only paper trading for strategies that failed section 6 (see above).
- Sector = NSE's industry from today's Nifty 500 list; NSE's archive has no history of
  it. Stocks outside the Nifty 500 have no sector and are not sector-capped (the signal
  says so).
- "3 banks != 3 separate trades": at most two held stocks that move together
  (correlation >= 0.7 over 120 sessions).
- Investing tier's 15 positions arrive with the first investing strategy (M6 needs
  fundamentals); the setting exists for swing and positional now.
- The 08:30 pre-open job is M7 ("Exit engine: pre-open checks"); M4 updates paper
  trades after the close with the next day's bar, as the backtest does.

## Tests
- `tests/test_risk_rules.py`: one test per spec section 5 rule (sizing formula, 20%
  cap, 1% of volume, risk setting limits and regime halving, max positions, sector cap,
  correlation, heat warn and block, drawdown halve and pause, breakeven at +1R, half at
  T1, 2 x ATR trail, time stops, costs in R). Some point at existing golden tests.
- Engine: paper options (no forced exit at the end, orders on the last day) leave the
  golden trades unchanged.
- Signals: schema validation rejects missing fields, a signal built from an order has
  the right zone, stop, targets, reward:risk and shares.
- Paper job on Postgres: opens accounts from backtest runs, records signals, fills and
  updates trades on the next day, is idempotent, refuses a blocked day, and serves the
  API.

## Acceptance checks
- `uv run pytest -q` covers every section 5 rule (list in `tests/test_risk_rules.py`).
- On real data: `paper` for two consecutive days records signals on day 1 and the
  fills/updates on day 2 (shown in the PR).
